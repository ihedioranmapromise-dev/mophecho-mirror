How to Use the Moph Echo Mirror

  • Speak your truth – Type or speak whatever is on your mind. It can be a feeling, a question, or a story.
  • The mirror listens – It identifies your Element (Fire, Water, Earth, Air), your spiritual Level, your Core Wound, and your Core Gift.
  • It asks deep questions – Each reflection is followed by a question to help you go deeper.
  • Golden Stars ✨ – When the mirror identifies a major trait, it will give you a golden star insight. These are stored in your Profile.
  • Profile – View your identified traits and all golden moments. Upload a profile picture by clicking the avatar.
  • Learning – The mirror remembers your past messages and avoids repeating the same response. If you ask "how" or "what do you mean," it will give you deeper wisdom.
  • Download – Use the ⤓ button to download your entire chat log as a text file.

— The mirror does not judge. It only reflects. —
